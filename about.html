About Empire Glassware

Who We Are

Empire Glassware is an Ahmedabad-based business focused on the wholesale and B2B supply of glassware, crockery, houseware, kitchenware, gifting products and vacuum bottles.

We work with retailers, wholesalers, gifting businesses, hospitality buyers and other commercial customers looking for a practical source of products for resale and bulk requirements.

Our approach is simple: make product discovery easier, provide clear product information, coordinate wholesale requirements through our sales team, and support dispatch for customers across Gujarat and other parts of India.
