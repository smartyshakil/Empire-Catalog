🏛️ Who We Are & Our Heritage

Headquartered in Ahmedabad, Empire Glassware stands tall as a recognized name in the wholesale import and distribution sector across Gujarat and India. For years, we have bridged the gap between global manufacturing excellence and domestic retail demand, supplying top-tier lifestyle merchandise that transforms dining tables and kitchens.

We specialize in bulk commercial imports, catering directly to wholesalers, large retail chains, gifting houses, and hospitality partners seeking unmatched quality at transparent dealer pricing.
